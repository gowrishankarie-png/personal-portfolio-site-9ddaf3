Ordem Press asked for a book that treated concrete with the reverence usually reserved for marble. I photographed 86 buildings across Portugal and Spain, then designed the book around their shadows.

## Sequencing

Spreads are paced like a walk through a building: compressed corridors of small images open suddenly onto full-bleed atriums. Vermilion is used only once per chapter — always on a door.

## Type

Headlines are cut in a heavy grotesque that sits on the page like poured slab; captions are set tiny and generous, so the photographs breathe.

## Outcome

Shortlisted for the Portuguese Book Design Awards and sold out its first print run in nine weeks.
