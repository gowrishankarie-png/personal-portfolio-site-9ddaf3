The museum's hall is a long, stubborn rectangle. Rather than fight it, we turned it into a forest of hanging linen, so visitors drift between photographs instead of marching along walls.

## Light

Each banner is lit from a single high spotlight, so prints glow against shadow the way they would in a darkroom tray.

## Graphics

Captions are printed on the linen itself in a quiet mono, keeping the room free of plaques and placards.

## Outcome

Extended twice, closing after 41,820 visitors — the museum's best-attended show of the decade.
