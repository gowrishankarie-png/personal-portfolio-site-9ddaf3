An open competition entry for a new line through the eastern city. The brief asked for something that would still look right in forty years.

## Material

Every sign is vitreous enamel — the same material Porto has used for street names for a century — so the system ages alongside the tiles around it.

## Numerals

Station numbers are drawn oversized and geometric, legible at 38 metres down a platform, and paired with pictograms based on what each neighbourhood is known for.

## Outcome

Awarded an honourable mention, and the pictogram set has since been adopted by two local transit operators.
