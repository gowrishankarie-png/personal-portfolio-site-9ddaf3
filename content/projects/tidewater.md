Tidewater's founders gather kelp by hand at low tide. They wanted a brand that felt as unhurried as that ritual — no glossy spa clichés, no stock waves.

## The idea

The mark is a single line that redraws itself across six states, one for each phase of the tide. Every product carries the state of the tide on the morning its batch was harvested, so no two shelves look identical.

## The system

A sea-glass green, a bone white and a deep kelp brown carry the whole palette. Typography is set in a narrow serif for product names and a mechanical mono for batch data, printed directly on uncoated stock that softens in the hand.

## Outcome

Launched across 37 independent stockists in the first year, with the batch labels becoming the thing customers photograph most.
